Frequently asked questions

Is Taalif NG free?

Many resources and practice questions are free. Some training and full mock exams are paid, and some places are sponsored. Each programme page shows its funding.

How do I pay?

Payments are in naira by card, bank transfer and other supported methods. Access opens only after your payment is confirmed on our servers.

Are CBT scores official?

No. Taalif mock scores help you practise and are separate from official JAMB, WAEC or NECO results.

Are your certificates accredited?

Certificates confirm attendance, completion or assessed competency in a Taalif programme. We state clearly when a programme has any external approval.

What happened to EduPulse?

Taalif NG replaces EduPulse. Eligible resources, exam content and learner accounts are being moved across with checks, and you will be invited to activate your account.

How is my data protected?

We collect only what we need, keep documents in private storage, and ask for guardian consent for minors where required.
